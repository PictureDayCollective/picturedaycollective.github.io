Common Questions

What if I can't make the live sessions?
Every session is recorded and added to the searchable archive. You can watch any session at any time. That said, the live Q&A is where you get your specific situation answered — so showing up when you can is worth it.
I'm just starting out — is this for me?
Yes. The foundation video library covers everything from how to pitch your first school to the basic lighting setup and order workflow. You don't need existing experience — you just need to be serious about building this part of your business.
Is this only for preschool, or does it cover other school photography?
The focus is preschool (including daycare) picture days specifically — the spaces, the logistics, the school relationships, the parent communication, the pricing. Much of it applies to other school photography, and many of our schools go through teens, but preschool is the core niche, for very specific reasons we'll detail in the community.
What does "member-directed curriculum" actually mean?
We'll have ongoing polls to request topic options. The topics with the most votes are what we cover. You are literally choosing what gets taught. No pre-set curriculum, no outdated module list.
Can I cancel?
Yes, any time. No contracts, no cancellation fees. If you're a founding member and you cancel, you'll rejoin at a new rate — so stay as long as it's valuable, which we expect to be a long time.
Why is the founding rate so much lower?
Founding members get rewarded for their commitment to jump starting the community. That deserves recognition. The $17 rate is locked permanently as a thank-you for being one of the first. It actually doesn't reflect the full value of the community, but future rates will.
