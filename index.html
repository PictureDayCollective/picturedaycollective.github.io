Common Questions

What if I can't make the live sessions?

Every session is recorded and added to the searchable archive. You can watch any session at any time. That said, the live Q&A is where you get your specific situation answered — so showing up when you can is worth it.

I'm just starting out — is this for me?

Yes. The foundation video library covers everything from how to pitch your first school to lighting setups and order workflow. You don't need existing experience — you just need to be serious about building this part of your business.

Is this only for preschool, or does it cover other school photography?

The focus is preschool picture days specifically — the spaces, the logistics, the school relationships, the parent communication, the pricing. Much of it applies to other volume school photography, but preschool is the core niche.

What does "member-directed curriculum" actually mean?

Before every session, a poll goes out to members with topic options. Whatever gets the most votes is what we cover that week. You are literally choosing what gets taught. No pre-set curriculum, no outdated module list.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. No contracts, no cancellation fees. If you're a founding member and you cancel, your founding rate is not guaranteed if you rejoin — so stay as long as it's valuable, which we expect to be a long time.

Why is the founding rate so much lower?

Founding members take a leap of faith on something new. That deserves recognition. The $27 rate is locked permanently as a thank-you for being one of the first. The regular rate of $47/month reflects the full value of the community once it's established.
